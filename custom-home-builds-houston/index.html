Questions

Custom Home Builds — FAQ

Do you build the whole house or just the exterior shell?

We specialize in the exterior shell plus exterior carpentry — the frame, floors, roof, dried-in envelope, and exterior cornice and trim. We coordinate with your GC and interior trades so the rest of the build flows. If you don't have a general contractor yet, we can point you toward builders we trust.

How much does a custom home build cost in Houston?

Total cost depends on the lot, design, and finish level, and it varies widely across Houston. Our exterior framing and carpentry package is priced per square foot from your plans — send the drawings and you'll have a written number within one business day.

How long does it take to get to dried-in?

For a typical Houston custom home, the shell runs several weeks from layout to weather-tight, depending on size, roof complexity, and inspection timing. You get a milestone schedule with your proposal and updates at every stage.

Do you handle permits and inspections?

Framing work happens under the permit held by you or your GC. We make sure our scope is drawn and built to current Houston code — including windstorm bracing — and we walk every framing inspection with the inspector until it passes.

Do you also do interior trim and finish work?

No — we focus on exterior framing and exterior carpentry. We don't do interior crown molding, interior trim, cabinets, or interior finishes. Your GC or a separate interior trim crew handles that scope.

Can I act as my own general contractor?

Yes — many of our clients are owner-builders. We help you sequence the trades, schedule inspections, and avoid the expensive mistakes first-time builders make. You keep control; we keep the structure on track.
