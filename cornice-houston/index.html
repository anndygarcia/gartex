What we build

Custom exterior cornice & architectural trim work

The exterior cornice is what gives a custom home its finished silhouette — the line where the wall meets the roof. Gartex fabricates and installs custom exterior cornice, built-up cornice profiles, frieze boards, brackets, rafter tails, and architectural detail carpentry for custom homes across Houston. Built to match your architect's drawings and engineered for the Houston climate.

We work from your architect's drawings or design the cornice with you on site. Every assembly is cut, fitted, and installed for tight joints and clean paint lines: exterior cornice returns, frieze and band trim, decorative brackets, column wraps, porch rafter tails, and gable detail work. We use rot- and termite-resistant materials (PVC, cedar, treated lumber, fiber cement) matched to the way your home is finished.

Questions

Exterior Cornice & Architectural Trim — FAQ

What's the difference between cornice and crown molding?

Crown molding is interior trim where the wall meets the ceiling. Cornice is the exterior architectural molding at the roofline — it can be a single profile or a built-up assembly with frieze, band, and brackets. Gartex specializes in exterior cornice and architectural trim; we don't do interior crown molding, interior trim work, or interior finishes.

How much does custom exterior cornice cost in Houston?

Cornice is priced per linear foot and depends on the profile complexity, material (PVC vs. cedar vs. fiber cement), wall height, and how much scaffolding or lift access the home needs. Send us your plans or invite us for an on-site measure — you'll get a written, line-item quote, typically within one business day.

What material should I use for exterior cornice?

PVC is the standard for zero-maintenance painted cornice — it doesn't rot, doesn't take termites, and holds paint well. Cedar is the choice when you want a stain-grade natural look. Fiber cement works on fire-zone builds and commercial-style exteriors. We'll recommend the right material for your home's exposure and finish.

Do you repair or replace damaged cornice?

Yes — we replace rotten or storm-damaged sections, rebuild failed built-up assemblies, and match the existing profile so the repair is invisible. Common in older Memorial, Tanglewood, and River Oaks homes.

How long does exterior cornice installation take?

A whole-house cornice package runs from a few days to a couple of weeks depending on linear footage, height, and detail. You get a schedule with your written estimate.
