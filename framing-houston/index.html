What we frame

Houston house framers who build it right the first time

Framing is the skeleton everything else hangs on — drywall, roofing, exterior finishes. Gartex Construction Company has framed custom homes across Houston for over a decade, and we treat every set of plans like the architect is going to walk the site with a tape measure. Straight walls, square corners, exact tolerances.

We work from your architectural drawings and handle everything from sill plate to dried-in shell: layout, wall framing, floor systems, roof framing, sheathing, and window and door openings set exactly where the plans say. Questions

Custom Home Framing — FAQ

How much does custom home framing cost in Houston?

Framing is usually priced per square foot and depends on your plans — roof complexity, ceiling heights, wood vs. steel, and second-story work all move the number. Send us your architectural drawings and we'll return a written, line-item estimate, typically within one business day.

How long does it take to frame a custom home?

A full custom home shell typically takes several weeks from layout to dried-in, depending on square footage, roof geometry, and inspection scheduling. Additions and second stories are faster. We give you a schedule with your estimate and keep your GC or architect updated at every stage.

Do you work with homeowners, or only general contractors and architects?

Both. Many of our jobs come from GCs and architects who need a framing crew they can trust, but we also work directly with homeowners building a custom home or planning a second-story addition. If you don't have plans yet, we can point you toward designers we work with.

What areas around Houston do you frame in?

We frame custom homes across Greater Houston — including Katy, Sugar Land, The Woodlands, Spring, Cypress, Pearland, Memorial, The Heights, River Oaks, Bellaire, and West University. Building outside that list? Call us — we likely go there too.

Is Gartex licensed and insured?

Yes. Gartex Construction Company carries full Texas general liability and workers' compensation coverage, and every job site is left broom-clean. Certificates of insurance are available for your GC or lender on request.
